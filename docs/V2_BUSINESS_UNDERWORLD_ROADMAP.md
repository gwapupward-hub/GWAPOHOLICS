# GWAPOHOLICS v2 — The Business Underworld
## Canonical vision and living build roadmap

**Founder direction locked:** November 18, 2026  
**Platform:** Telegram Mini App only  
**Status:** Approved *product concept and phased roadmap*; implementation sequencing, economy figures, release gates and dates remain subject to testing and approval. **This document is not authorization to merge, deploy, change production databases, or open a public beta.**  
**Working principle:** Build → Test → Play → Learn → Improve → Expand.  
**Exclusive active delivery scope:** **v2.1 — Build Your Empire**. All other numbered phases are future roadmap context, NOT authorized or active implementation. The final v2.1 build must demonstrate readiness to implement v2.2 The Deal Room. Any move to v2.2 requires a separate founder decision.

> A cinematic, persistent, multiplayer business universe where players earn their start, build enterprises, negotiate deals, compete for customers, adapt to a living economy, and gain or lose reputation because choices have consequences.

### 1. Non-negotiable product identity

GWAPOHOLICS v2 evolves the founder-approved v1 cinematic business-education journey into an interconnected business world, not an unrelated clicker/idle game, a gambling product, a cash-out economy, or a generic financial dashboard. Every future feature must meaningfully strengthen at least one of eight foundation pillars:

1. **Build Your Empire:** start with small businesses (barbershops, restaurants, fashion, logistics) and grow toward corporations; operating costs, customers, inventory and risk matter.
2. **The Deal Room:** player-negotiated partnerships, simulated investments, contracts and profit splits with enforceable in-game outcomes.
3. **The GWAP Exchange:** fictional marketplace for inventory and business assets, with supply and demand affected by real player activity.
4. **Corporate Wars:** competition for customers through marketing, supplier deals, positioning and legitimate acquisitions, not reflex-based combat.
5. **The World Keeps Moving:** changing economic conditions, disruptions, regulations and trends across the simulated economy.
6. **Pitch Battles:** founders pitch startup ideas to player-investors, who evaluate valuation, risk and business models.
7. **GWAP Syndicates:** Telegram-friend business crews, joint ventures, pooled resources, weekly cooperative challenges and collective reputation.
8. **Your Choices Follow You:** persistent, contextual reputation affected by fulfilling agreements, debt management, business stewardship and reliability.

**Creative presentation:** full-screen, cinematic, story-mode scenes; narrated character dialogue with typewriter bubbles; fades, gradual zooms, sequential props and consequences that appear in the world. Continue to support mobile-safe areas, accessibility and reduced-motion settings. Complex systems should feel like a story unfolding, not a wall of accounting forms.

**Learning promise:** realistic business decision-making; risk, financial literacy and trade-offs; no guarantee of profit. All G is fictional, non-transferable and has no cash value. Solana tokenization, withdrawals, swaps and real-world redemption remain **future planning, not approved features**.

### 2. The player's canonical journey

1. **Origin (v1 qualification):** new players start their *qualification account* at **0 G**; play the five existing cinematic educational quests and receive a verified full-run Origin Score to become their v2 startup capital.
2. **Graduation:** enter The Business Underworld with credited capital and an initial founder record. Do not conflate a qualification score with lifetime earnings or actual available cash.
3. **First enterprise:** open a small starter operation or, if capital is insufficient, complete bounded educational starter opportunities; earn through decisions and manage expenses.
4. **Economic survival:** make choices about inventory, pricing, marketing, staffing, customer demand, solvency and expansion.
5. **Social strategy:** negotiate with humans, compete, seek investment, form partnerships and later establish syndicates.
6. **Long-term identity:** wealth can rise or fall, but history, commitments and context-sensitive reputation persist.

**Preserve prior founder-approved replay rule:** unlimited v1 replays anytime, including after v2 entry; only the positive difference above the highest *previously credited eligible full-run score* grants additional v2 capital. Equal or worse scores grant 0 G. Replays must never erase v2 money, companies, contracts or reputation. Credit score improvement exactly once under an atomic server transaction. Existing v1 testers, balances, quests and recovery state must not be silently converted or wiped; migration/opt-in policy needs explicit approval.

**Economy/level draft boundary:** PR #47 (Zero-to-Empire economy, XP, safe gates) contains *proposed* numeric caps, progression and code, not approved production settings. Its anti-farming principles, transactional receipts and nontransferability are dependencies. No new exact G prices, XP thresholds or exchange permissions are hereby approved.

#### Founder Entry foundation additions — 2.1-A / early v2.1 (founder direction, November 18, 2026)

The following six requirements are part of **v2.1**, not a new numbered phase or permission to build v2.2. Include their **contracts and the A-level experience in 2.1-A**; extend and use the contracts during 2.1-B through 2.1-E. Keep v1 story gameplay and existing tester data unchanged. This is a roadmap/specification change only; no live GwapScore data sync, database migration, merge or deployment is authorized.

1. **Origin → GwapScore / verifiable founder reputation (A foundation; extend in C/D).** Create one durable `founder_id` tied server-side to authenticated Telegram identity and an append-only, versioned evidence stream of verified Origin runs, meaningful decisions, graduations, reversals/corrections and later business actions. Keep **Origin Score (qualification/performance)** distinct from **reliability/reputation (behavior supported by evidence)**; high capital is not proof of trustworthiness. Persist internal reputation facts from day one, with verifiable event/receipt IDs, policy version, timestamps and evidence digests. Define a read-only, permission-scoped GwapScore-compatible export/attestation adapter so proof can be linked without reworking identity later. **No automatic external GwapScore linkage, publication, wallet association or sharing of private quest histories until separate privacy/security review and affirmative player consent**; where not approved, the equivalent in-game founder reputation record is the authoritative implementation. Support correction/revocation events rather than rewriting history.

2. **Minimal shared-world visibility (A).** Add a server-provided, read-only `Active Founders` directory or small leaderboard inside the Founder Hub. Profiles are **private by default** and require an explicit opt-in to appear, with an easy opt-out; show only an approved display alias, non-sensitive founder milestone and coarse activity indicator. Never expose raw Telegram IDs, wallet addresses, exact private financial balances, authentication data, or full decision histories. Use server-derived participation, bounded pagination/rate limits and moderation controls; do not equate wealth rank with reliability. No messaging, bidding, offers, asset transfers or player interactions in A.

3. **Failure and recovery (A, with deeper business recovery in C/D).** A valid five-quest Origin completion with **0 G** or too little capital still enters the Founder Hub; display an explicit `Zero Capital` / `Insufficient Starting Capital` state and the next eligible action. Provide unlimited complete v1 replays; grant only positive improvements over the already credited best score. Expose a clear route to **bounded educational side activities** as a future or feature-flagged recovery source, backed by server-verified receipts, frequency limits and a documented inflation budget; do not create uncapped farming in A. The legacy 100 G gameplay lock must **never** prevent Origin qualification, safe browsing or approved educational recovery. Do not silently top up, reset v2 progress or grant capital for incomplete runs.

4. **Funnel and economy telemetry (A).** Instrument server-side, versioned events for `founder_first_login`, `origin_quest_completed`, `origin_run_finalized`, `origin_capital_credit_committed` (including zero-delta outcomes separately), `founder_entered_v2`, `founder_directory_viewed`, `recovery_path_viewed`, and `recovery_completed`. Record event ID, UTC timestamp, anonymous/pseudonymous analytic player key, cohort/feature version, source receipt/run reference, funnel stage and safe outcome/score band; ensure idempotent emission/replay handling and explicitly documented retention/access controls. **No raw Telegram identifiers, wallet addresses, quest answers or sensitive private records in third-party analytics**. Analytics are observational, not an authority for crediting G. Review qualification completion, zero/low-capital frequency, credit delta distribution, recovery selection, founder-hub entry and first-business interest before tuning B; consolidated manual QA/acceptance remains at the end of 2.1 per founder direction.

5. **Immutable capital audit trail (A).** For **every** finalized Origin run, save an immutable outcome/settlement receipt; for **every positive capital credit**, append an immutable G ledger entry containing `transaction_id`, `founder_id`, `origin_run_id`, verified `origin_score_G`, `previous_best_G`, `credit_delta_G`, `best_after_G`, policy/version, `occurred_at_utc`, and idempotency key. A zero-delta outcome records a non-credit settlement receipt, **not** a zero-value monetary credit. Enforce unique run settlement, atomic best-score update + balance grant + receipt, safe concurrent retries, non-negative balances and exact reconciliation; never edit a past credit to "fix" it—append a compensating correction with a reference and authorization trail. Expose a privacy-scoped receipt/history view for player support and controlled audits.

6. **Three starter business category stubs (A contract; playable in B).** Register **barbershop (services)**, **food market stall (retail)**, and **online fashion shop (commerce)** as versioned, **inactive/non-purchasable** templates. Each needs `business_type_id`, name/category, `setup_cost_G`, `minimum_operating_reserve_G`, expected per-cycle operating/supply costs, capacity, customer/demand model inputs, potential gross margin, risk/variance range, inventory applicability, and content/art hooks. For an *illustrative planning baseline only*, adapt unapproved PR #47 tiers as: food market stall **250 G setup + 75 G reserve**; online fashion shop **500 G + 150 G**; barbershop **850 G + 250 G** (service-agency tier proxy). **These figures are hypotheses, not approved prices or deployable defaults**; require balancing, explicit founder approval and versioned server configuration before activation. During A show a preview of possible paths without charging or creating businesses; in B activate only the first economically viable category while preserving all IDs/schema for additional categories. Track `business_type_previewed` as an A telemetry event.

**2.1-A implementation order and automated acceptance:** First establish the isolated identity/origin/ledger and evidence contracts; then add first-login/quest/credit telemetry; then zero-capital recovery entry and the opt-in read-only Founder Hub; finally add disabled business templates and their API/content contracts. Test duplicate and concurrent credit requests, 0/lower/equal/higher replay outcomes, privacy opt-in/opt-out and authorization, observability deduplication, and disabled-template purchase rejection in isolated CI. Preserve old v1 player state and defer manual cohort/balance/release sign-off to the consolidated end-of-v2.1 gate. No main/default Neon writes, live bot changes or production release.

#### Retention-first v2.1 scope addendum — return tomorrow, not only finish today (founder direction, November 18, 2026)

**North-star retention question:** "What changed while I was away, and what choice should I make now?" This addendum extends **existing v2.1 builds A–E**; it does not authorize a second game, idle-clicker economy or any feature from v2.2 onward. Build F still provides the non-live v2.2 technical handoff. No new G or XP amounts, live cohorts, or production changes are approved.

1. **Founder Goal Ladder (A; maintain through D):** Show **3–5 clear, named milestone cards** on the Founder Hub. Initial example sequence: `Complete the Origin`, `Open your first company`, `Complete your first operating cycle`, `Make a profitable decision`, `Keep your business solvent across operating cycles`. While A precedes business activation, future steps show **locked or upcoming**, never falsely completed or collectible. Goals derive solely from server-verified immutable quest, company, operations and solvency receipts; version the goals and make completion idempotent. Each card shows progress, why it matters and one next action. Avoid daily chores, paid accelerators, streak punishment and unapproved G/XP bonuses. Update dynamically in B/C/D as the existing events become real.

2. **Decision echoes (B origin, D callback):** During the first starter business setup, offer **one** meaningful fictional NPC supplier or financing-choice scenario with explicit trade-offs (cost, reliability, service or constraints); persist a `founder_decision` receipt with a stable choice/callback ID and policy version. D reuses the same receipt in at least one later operating narrative/outcome, including a short "Because you chose X ..." explanation and a Founder Hub decision-history entry. Keep business performance separate from behavioral reputation. **No real lender, debt product, player-to-player loan/contract or multi-party settlement**. The callback is a bounded story/business rule, not an early Deal Room.

3. **Capped offline operating simulation + next-visit report (C):** A previously opened, solvent **operating business** may simulate customer demand, sales and expenses during elapsed time away, limited by a **server-configured maximum eligible duration**, per-business operating capacity, inventory/supplies, available working capital and a bounded profit/loss envelope. Use trusted server UTC timestamps and a persisted `last_settled_at` checkpoint; evaluate the *net result* of revenue **minus** wages, rent, supplies and other applicable costs under a versioned policy. A period can earn **zero or negative net profit**; never promise guaranteed G or passive APY. Do not grant passive welcome coins, tap rewards, bot-mining yields, staking returns, fixed interest, token rewards, or unlimited catch-up income. No accrual until the business has completed its first authorized operating setup; stop when resource or solvency conditions prevent operations.

   Settle only on an authenticated server action (such as return/snapshot) in a **single transaction**: lock the founder and business, clamp time to policy limits, calculate bounded deterministic periods from persisted facts, append per-period revenue/expense and net ledger entries, advance checkpoint, and store a unique settlement/report receipt. Duplicate requests, multiple Vercel instances, device time edits, repeated logins, and stale clients must not generate extra income. Restrict the number/frequency of catch-up windows; there is no automatic indefinite accumulation beyond the cap. Include operational outages/force majeure as pause rules. No client-calculated G.

   On the next visit, display one short cinematic **"Since you were away" report**: time simulated, customers/orders, revenue, explicit costs, net gain/loss, operational status, the most relevant consequence and **one decision** to make today. A report can be viewed again without re-settling or multiplying earnings. "Morning report" is a UX metaphor; users receive it on their next open, not a scheduled push notification or guaranteed local-time delivery. Use reduced-motion support and keep the report intelligible without animations. Log `offline_period_settled`, `return_report_presented`, and `return_report_decision_committed` against immutable receipts. Operational caps and rates require playtest/economic approval before enablement.

4. **Bankruptcy and guided comeback (D, grounded in A recovery):** Failure is a *business lifecycle state*, not deletion of founder identity. Record the insolvency cause, verified losses, decision history and **one clear learning takeaway**. A founder who loses their first business gets access to a finite recovery journey (e.g., a bounded educational contract or one-off restart assistance after a real learning decision) that results in an **approved, reduced-but-nonzero starting stake** for reopening; protect against indefinite replayable grants and scripted intentional bankruptcy. Use per-insolvency idempotency keys, eligibility/velocity limits, anti-abuse rules and a budgeted G source recorded in the capital/business ledger. A player at 0 G can always view their history, replay Origin and perform eligible no-cost learning. Do not silently refill operating losses, erase prior businesses, reset the best Origin score, rewrite economic history or bypass solvency and 100 G risk-action rules. Exact recovery amounts and reset eligibility are tuning hypotheses pending founder approval.

5. **Retention telemetry begins in A (events activate as their gameplay ships):** In A, define and instrument **`session_started`**, `founder_first_login`, `origin_quest_completed`, `origin_capital_credit_committed`, `founder_goal_viewed`, and an identity-safe return-session/cohort model. Define **`first_company_opened`** in A but emit only after B successfully commits a company's creation; define **`first_operating_loss`** in A but emit only when C commits the first verified negative net operating cycle; add `offline_period_settled`, `return_report_presented`, `return_report_decision_committed` and `comeback_completed` as the associated mechanics ship. Distinguish sessions from refresh/retry noise using authenticated server session IDs and bounded inactivity rules. Track **day-2 / next-day return** as the percentage of first-day founder cohorts with a server-verified return session on the next UTC calendar day (also keep a separate rolling 24–48-hour measure for interpretation); state the denominator, timezone, exclusions and cohort size. Use pseudonymous analytics keys, unique event/receipt IDs, a retention policy and no third-party raw Telegram IDs, private histories or monetary-identifying fields. A's event schema is not a substitute for authoritative transactional records. Review measurement output from internal builds; the previously locked **consolidated manual QA/economic acceptance remains at the end of 2.1**, not per sprint.

**Offline-economy anti-inflation acceptance:** Simulate profitable/unprofitable/closed/underfunded businesses, absence durations past the cap, concurrent logins, rollback/retry and clock-boundary cases. Test that accumulated *gross* revenue and costs reconcile with the persistent ledger and that net G creation stays inside policy bounds across daily cohorts. Daily login must **not** be rewarded with unconditional minting, and multiple sessions must not affect elapsed-time rewards. If the system cannot prove bounded settlement, keep the offline earning flag disabled rather than introducing a competing idle-income subsystem.

#### v2.1 completion / v2.2 entry contract

All of the following must be true before the founder is asked to authorize implementation of **v2.2 — The Deal Room**:

1. **Playable continuity and retention:** A player completes v1 qualification, enters v2.1, sees the goal ladder, opens a business, records a consequential first decision, runs active and capped offline operating cycles, returns to a cost/revenue report requiring one decision, later encounters a prior-choice callback, upgrades or recovers from insolvency, and retains founder/company/history state across sessions.
2. **Money integrity:** Only authenticated server actions can change G, ownership, cost/revenue, or progression; Origin, offline periods and bounded comeback credits have atomic, idempotent, auditable receipts and are zero-duplicate under concurrency. Offline earnings must reconcile **both costs and revenue**, respect time/resource/solvency caps, and never act as token yield.
3. **Deal-ready entities:** Durable, unique player/company/ownership identifiers; permission boundaries; a documented business lifecycle; immutable or versioned financial statements and event history; enough data to value or assess a counterparty.
4. **Extension-safe interfaces:** Versioned APIs and database migration strategy make it possible to add offers, two-party consent, agreement terms, expiry, obligations, profit splits and transaction settlement **without rewriting v2.1 records**. These are schema/contract readiness requirements, **not** permission to activate those features.
5. **Reputation foundation:** Founder/Origin event receipts start in A, distinguish economic performance from reliability and have explainable correction paths. The GwapScore-compatible read-only export contract is documented and privacy-gated; any external linking requires affirmative opt-in and separate privacy/security approval. Future agreement fulfillment can extend the event vocabulary without rewriting history.
6. **Tested future seam:** A non-live two-player mock/contract test demonstrates identities, permissions, funds availability and expected settlement boundaries. It does not move G or expose a negotiating UI.
7. **Operational and player evidence:** Closed tester results, mobile Telegram QA, abuse/rate controls, documented rollback and verified restore tests; no unaddressed critical blockers.
8. **Explicit founder approval:** Review final acceptance record and grant **separate approval** to start v2.2. Completing 2.1 does not automatically launch 2.2 or authorize a production deployment.

### 5. Universal definition of done for each v2.1 build and future phase

1. **Player value:** the build has a five-minute *meaningful next step* (or a clearly justified longer intentional learning loop), with cinematic explanation and visible consequences.
2. **Integrity:** authenticated Telegram identity; server-controlled balances, ownership and outcomes; idempotency keys and atomic multi-party transactions where required; no spoofed client "profits".
3. **Continuity:** previous balances, Origin best score, companies, contracts and reputation persist without involuntary resets; feature-flagged migrations are reversible and tested in isolated storage.
4. **Economy:** simulate creation/sinks of G, insolvency, recovery, exploit paths and player collusion; verify no infinite replay grants, negative balances, duplicate payouts or unbacked asset transfers.
5. **Player safety:** clear simulated economy labels, accessible UX, fraud and rate limits, reporting/dispute mechanism appropriate to feature complexity; no real-money conversion.
6. **Operations:** production-like monitoring, incident response, restore evidence and data protection; release candidate checks green and manual Telegram WebView QA recorded.
7. **Forward readiness:** tests/documentation prove the next v2.1 build's prerequisites, and the final 2.1 build proves the explicit 2.2 handoff contract without shipping 2.2 gameplay.
8. **Founder gate:** branch/PR reviewed and explicitly approved for merge or deployment. Design approval does **not** override this step.
